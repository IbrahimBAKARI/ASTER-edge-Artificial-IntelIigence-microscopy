# Progressive OOD degradation results

- AML / blur: median 454.05 to 3778.66; rejection 20% to 100%; median Spearman rho 0.943; 30% of trajectories non-decreasing.
- AML / color: median 454.05 to 578.55; rejection 20% to 50%; median Spearman rho 0.971; 50% of trajectories non-decreasing.
- Stem-cell donor / blur: median 467.86 to 3649.45; rejection 5% to 100%; median Spearman rho 1.000; 55% of trajectories non-decreasing.
- Stem-cell donor / color: median 467.86 to 571.01; rejection 5% to 45%; median Spearman rho 1.000; 65% of trajectories non-decreasing.

A higher final score does not establish monotonicity for every patient. This
experiment measures sensitivity to synthetic transformations of public cell
crops; it does not demonstrate detection of a specific enclosure or microscope.
